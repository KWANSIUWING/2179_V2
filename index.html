Malaysia Cities/States Overview

Economy, Population and Other Aspects

Preface

This page aims to introduce the overview of Malaysia

cities and states to those people who are interested in

the geography of Malaysia.

Introduction

To show the overview of Malaysia cities and states,

we should look at many aspects. First, we can see

the scale of these cities and states.

Population(city)

Which Malaysia city has the largest population?

When it comes to city, It is clear that Kuala Lumpur

has the largest population. Moreover, it also has the

fastest population growth among these 6 cities, while

that of the bottom 3 cities are similar, and very low.

Area VS Population(state)

Which Malaysia state has the largest area?

Even though Sarawak has the largest area, its

population per area is not as large as others.

Which Malaysia city has the largest area?

We can find that Johor bahru has the largest are,

while Kuala Lumpur ranks only fourth.

Population Density(city)

Which Malaysia city has the largest population density?

According to the map on right side,

we can see that Kuala Lumpur has the

largest population density. In descending

order of population density, these cities

are Kuala Lumpur, George Town, Johor Bahru,

Ipoh, Kuching, Kota Kinabalu.

Review

Conventionally, we regard Kuala Lumpur as the
